About me

Born in Saint Petersburg, grew up in Ukhta and Shchyolkovo. Into aviation since school: themed camps at Orlyonok and Artek, research projects on aerodynamics and composites.

More

The plan: graduate in 2029, work as an engineer in Germany, and fund modular EASA fATPL training from the salary.
